2 Data We Collect & Why

ZONE supports instant guest play with no login wall. We collect only the minimal data required to operate gameplay and optional cloud sync:

  • Optional Account Details: If you choose to create or link a cloud profile via the Account portal, we store your optional email address, operative handle/username, and authentication session token so you can recover progress across devices.
  • Gameplay Progression Stats: Operative level, virtual cash, battle stats, crimes, gym training, inventory, merits, honors, medals, faction membership, and territory actions.
  • Local Device & Browser Preferences: Theme choice (Light Paper / Dark Charcoal), interface language (English / Arabic), layout preferences, and offline PWA cache stored locally on your device.
  • Zero Sale & Zero Ad Tracking: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data. ZONE contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no ad identifiers, and no cross-app behavioral trackers.

3 Third-Party Infrastructure Services

ZONE relies on trusted infrastructure providers strictly to deliver the application and 3D city map:

  • Vercel: Provides HTTPS hosting, global edge CDN delivery, and serverless API execution. Standard server access logs (such as IP address and request timestamps) may be processed transiently for reliability, rate limiting, and DDoS mitigation.
  • Mapbox: Serves vector and 3D map tiles powering the interactive ZONE City territory map.

We do not grant any third party permission to use your data for targeted advertising.

4 Account & Data Deletion (30-Day SLA)

You may request permanent deletion of your ZONE operative account and all associated personal data at any time using either method below:

  • Direct Email Request: Email weni.mohamed@gmail.com with the subject "ZONE Account Deletion Request (com.zonecity.syndicate)" and your operative handle or registered email address.

What gets deleted & timeline: Upon verification, we permanently erase your account credentials, optional email address, active session tokens, and synced cloud gameplay profile within 30 days. Local browser/app cache can also be cleared instantly from your device settings. Minimal anonymized security or anti-abuse logs may be retained only where legally required.

All communications between the ZONE app/browser and our servers are encrypted in transit using modern HTTPS / TLS protected by HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS), strict X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN headers.
