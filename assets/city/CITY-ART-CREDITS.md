# ZONE harbor city artwork, revision 83

Runtime file: `assets/city/zone-harbor-city-v83.webp`.

Original artwork generated for this ZONE project with the built-in image generation tool on 9 October 2026. It is not Torn art, satellite photography, an OpenStreetMap tile, or a downloaded third-party city map. No ownership colors, emblems, text, territory boundaries or game rules are baked into the image.

The prompt requested a coherent orthographic urban crime RPG harbor district: a diagonal urban spine, old-quarter courtyards, central park, civic dome/plaza, distinct downtown towers, hospital campus, railway station/yard, freight docks, factories, rowhouses, stadium, believable streets, roof equipment, neutral masonry and asphalt, muted green parks and slate water. It explicitly excluded repetitive grids, UI, labels, ownership overlays, hatching, connectors, logos and watermarks.

The requested higher output resolution was not delivered by the generation tool. The actual inspected output is 1122 by 1402 pixels. It is encoded as WebP at quality 91, 697,964 bytes, with SHA256 `4EA43FD0D8480F1E9B71186AE9952C3E5B62F3D6CFA50E43F35D81038A0BC765`. Automatic cameras respect a 1.35 CSS-pixel maximum per artwork texel. Manual zoom retains its existing full interaction range.

The raster uses the exact Mercator bounding rectangle of the authoritative 120 territory polygons. Its scenery is cosmetic. The independent engine territory source supplies hit testing, identities, ownership, adjacency, action eligibility, costs and active conflicts. The image has no claim authority.

The perspective control tilts this aerial artwork. This revision does not claim the illustrated buildings are new 3D geometry. Diagnostics report `original-city-art` and `tilted-art`. The native geometry remains a local fallback for failed artwork loading. The retained optional KayKit assets have their separate license in `assets/city/kaykit/LICENSE.txt`.

References checked during the implementation:

- [Mapbox sources and raster image coordinates](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/guides/styles/work-with-layers/): image corners follow top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left order.
- [Mapbox source API](https://docs.mapbox.com/mapbox-gl-js/api/sources/): image sources and local GeoJSON remain separate render inputs.
- [Torn city map announcement](https://www.torn.com/forums.php?p=threads&t=16575107): current public reference for map interaction and accessibility, not permission to reuse Torn art.
- [Kenney licensing](https://kenney.nl/support) and [City Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban): evaluated CC0 asset option. No new Kenney models were downloaded for this revision because another repeated modular city kit would not solve the rejected visual direction.
- [OSM tile usage policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/): evaluated hosted basemap option. No OSM tile dependency was added.
