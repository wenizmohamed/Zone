# ZONE overhead city atlas

The atlas in `atlas.js` is original procedural artwork authored for ZONE in 2026.
Its asphalt, rooftops, HVAC units, roof lights, crosswalks, service yards, trees,
parking lines and street lamps are drawn from source code. It uses no downloaded
images, real-world satellite photography, Torn artwork, or third-party textures.

The server supplies territory polygons and district display names. The cosmetic
generator cannot assign owners, issue combat actions, accrue income, or change
the authoritative 120-block catalog. The existing optional tilted city view
continues to use the credited KayKit City Bits models in `kaykit/`.
