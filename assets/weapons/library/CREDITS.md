## Processing

Source geometry was retained. FBX/OBJ and Draco GLB files were converted with Blender4.5.9. Embedded images were capped at1024px and opaque images encoded as JPEG90 when smaller. The source archives, original hashes and license-page evidence remain in the local acquisition record.

Models are a mixed-style collection: realistic textured weapons, stylized firearms, fantasy/medieval weapons, improvised melee weapons and complete emplacements. This is a gallery asset collection; character grip positions have not been calibrated for these424 models.
