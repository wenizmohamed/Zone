# ZONE M4 presentation derivative116

Original model and surface maps: 3DModelsCC0, [Free CC0 Guns & Explosives Pack](https://3dmodelscc0.itch.io/free-cc0-guns-explosives-pack). The primary author page explicitly offers its 19-model pack under the CC0 public-domain dedication and lists M4A1. That page was inspected on 2026-10-19. The retained normalized source and acquisition mirror are fingerprinted in manifest.json. CC0 does not grant trademark, patent or endorsement rights.

This visual-only derivative retains the source silhouette, all 9236 source triangles, UVs, and measured handle/scale/orientation. ZONE adds fitted chamfered receiver surfaces, individual rail hardware and cover treads, pin/selector detail, magazine grooves/floorplate, and stock/grip contact detail. Source surface wear, normal and metallic-roughness lineage remain. The source atlas receives a restrained albedo lift and 512-pixel filtering; the added steel, polymer and rubber kit uses original deterministic 256-pixel base-color, normal and metallic-roughness maps. Added geometry and new surface work are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal in LICENSE.txt. These are nonfunctional game-art meshes, not firearm engineering plans or an exact real-world-performance claim.

Only Assault Rifle I, III and V are rebound. IDs, stats, prices, owned stock and gameplay rules are unchanged. All source assets, 51 historical PNGs, and previous 111/112 derivatives are retained. One derivative is intentionally reused by the 3 existing M4-backed identities, not presented as 3 newly authored silhouettes.

The transparent PNG is an offline Blender 4.5.9 CPU Cycles render of the exported and re-imported GLB. Before/after evidence uses the same camera, lighting, frame and tone transform. These are not WebGL browser captures. Actual inventory/equipped presentation, phone performance and animated finger-skin clearance remain separate browser review gates. No premium/AAA acceptance is implied by an asset parse or offline render.
