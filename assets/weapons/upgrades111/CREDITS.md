# ZONE weapon visual derivatives 111

These files replace seven fictional gameplay item presentations, not item statistics or equipment rules. Every approved original model and the v99 viewer-capture receipt is retained unchanged.

## Original geometry

- G3A3 and L85A1: Tabasco, [Various Small Arms](https://opengameart.org/content/various-small-arms-assault-rifles-sniper-pistol). The primary author page offers CC0 and records the author's public-domain dedication dated 2023-12-15. The page also retains its earlier CC-BY marker. Exact retained v99 GLB inputs and acquisition archive hashes are recorded in manifest.json.
- Glock-18: locarem, [High Poly Glock-18 (No-Textures)](https://opengameart.org/content/high-poly-glock-18-no-textures), CC0. Downloaded from the author's OGA attachment, with its SHA256 in manifest.json. The source OBJ has 15,366 vertices and original slide, frame and control regions. The author did not supply textures or an MTL file. This is a new source for Pistol II/VI, not an M1911 relabel.

## ZONE modifications

The rifle models retain source silhouettes and measured grip placement. New bevelled hard-surface geometry adds receiver panels, reinforcing seams, fasteners, recessed-looking vents, magazine channels and grip/buttpad detail. The new Glock retains locarem's geometry with normalized physical scale and grip, small chamfers, grip panels and slide-serration detail. These are visual-only, nonfunctional game meshes, not production firearm designs or engineering specifications.

All new base-color, tangent-normal and packed metallic/roughness maps are deterministic procedural surface work for this derivative. There are no external photographic textures, AI-generated weapon meshes, manufacturer logos, or claims of original-author PBR maps. Worn steel is metallic; polymer and rubber remain nonmetallic with their own roughness. New ZONE derivative contributions are dedicated under CC0 1.0, included in LICENSE.txt. CC0 does not grant trademark or patent rights or imply manufacturer endorsement.

## Rendering and provenance boundary

The three PNG thumbnails are offline Blender 4.5.9 CPU Cycles renders of the exact exported mesh/material scene. They are not browser captures. Each before/after evidence pair uses one shared camera, framing, lights and charcoal background. Evidence and raw source remain under excluded .tools/source-tools locations, not this release folder. manifest.json records asset/thumbnail hashes, budgets, bindings and immutable originals.

Actual in-app WebGL preview, EN/AR phone layout, finger-skin clearance, and device performance require separate browser review. The manifest's browser status is pending until actual evidence exists. Historical assets/weapons/runtime-coverage.json remains a v99 receipt, never evidence for these new models.
