# ZONE sniper presentation derivatives 112

Original geometry: Tabasco, [Various Small Arms](https://opengameart.org/content/various-small-arms-assault-rifles-sniper-pistol). The primary author page offers CC0 and explicitly records a public-domain dedication on 2023-12-15; a historical CC-BY marker remains on that page. The retained v99 inputs are tabasco-dragunov.glb and tabasco-mosin9130.glb, with hashes and original source IDs in manifest.json. All originals remain unchanged.

ZONE modifications retain the source silhouettes and exact measured grip/scale normalization. The Dragunov adds receiver panels, seams, fasteners, ventilation accents, magazine channels, optic collars, clamps, lens discs and turret detail. The Mosin adds receiver/bolt-raceway surfaces, fasteners, iron-sight graduations and stock-band detail, preserving the original iron-sight configuration. Actual added detail triangles are recorded separately from triangles created by base-mesh chamfers. These are nonfunctional game-art meshes, not firearm production designs or mechanical specifications.

The wood color/normal maps retain LonesomeDucky's [CC0 old wooden baseball bat](https://opengameart.org/content/old-wooden-baseball-bat) lineage from the reviewed v99 adaptation. Original projected UVs and its restrained walnut factor preserve the richer worn grain; new ZONE nonmetallic roughness supplies readable surface breakup. ZONE's new steel and rubber base-color, tangent-normal and packed metallic/roughness maps are deterministic original procedural work. The Tabasco source had no texture maps, and this release does not claim original-author PBR. Optic lenses are an opaque coated-glass approximation with no through-scope functionality. No manufacturer branding or endorsement is asserted. CC0 does not grant trademark or patent rights.

ZONE derivative contributions are dedicated under CC0 1.0 Universal, included in LICENSE.txt. Manifest provenance and the binding proposal are additive. Historical runtime-coverage.json and existing source files are not rewritten.

The transparent PNG thumbnails are offline Blender 4.5.9 CPU Cycles renders of the exported GLBs after re-import, not browser captures. Full before/after and explicitly cropped detail pairs use identical cameras, framing, lighting and tone transforms per pair; their actual render camera metadata is in excluded .tools/v112-weapons. Production helpers remain under excluded source-tools/upgrades112. Raw sources and evidence PNGs are not public release assets.

The binding proposal covers only sniperrifle_2/_5 and sniperrifle_3/_6. Runtime/catalog integration, browser visual acceptance, phone GPU performance, and full animated finger-skin clearance remain separate and unverified by offline asset renders.
