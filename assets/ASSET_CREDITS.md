# ZONE asset credits

## Assets added in the 4 October 2026 visual overhaul

### City models
KayKit City Builder Bits 1.0 by **Kay Lousberg**. CC0 1.0 Universal.
Source: https://github.com/KayKit-Game-Assets/KayKit-City-Builder-Bits-1.0
License included at `city/kaykit/LICENSE.txt`.
Selected models: building A, C, E, G without base; sedan; streetlight; water tower; bush.

Existing weapon catalog and rigged character assets were retained. Their source comments identify Quaternius Ultimate Gun Pack and Universal Animation Library 2 respectively. This overhaul does not establish new licensing provenance for those pre-existing files, or download new Quaternius packs. Any broader redistribution should use the original pack's included license.
